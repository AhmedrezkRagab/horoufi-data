حروفي تطبيق لتعليم القراءة العربية للأطفال. التطبيق مبيطلبش تسجيل دخول ولا حساب ولا أي بيانات شخصية، وكل تقدّم الطفل بيتخزّن على الجهاز نفسه بس. البيانات الوحيدة اللي بتخرج من الجهاز هي اللي بتجمعها شبكة الإعلانات، وهي معرّفات جهاز مش هوية شخص.

٣. اللي بيخرج من الجهاز فعلًا

أ. الإعلانات (Google AdMob)

التطبيق بيعرض إعلانات من Google AdMob. زي أي شبكة إعلانات، بتستقبل بيانات تقنية عن الجهاز — أهمها معرّف الإعلانات وعنوان الـIP ونوع الجهاز ونظام التشغيل والتفاعل مع الإعلان.

مهم: لأن التطبيق موجّه للأطفال، إحنا مفعّلين إعدادات الأطفال في AdMob بشكل إجباري ومن غير استثناء:

  • إعلانات غير مخصّصة بس — ممنوع الإعلان المبني على سلوك الطفل أو اهتماماته
  • ممنوع الـremarketing — يعني مفيش تتبّع للطفل عبر التطبيقات والمواقع
  • تصنيف محتوى «G» — إعلانات مناسبة لكل الأعمار بس

سياسة خصوصية جوجل: policies.google.com/privacy

Privacy Policy — Horoufi (English)

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Horoufi is an Arabic reading app for children. The app requires no sign-in, no account, and no personal information. All of a child's progress is stored only on the device. The only data that leaves the device is collected by the advertising network, and it identifies a device, not a person.

1. Who we are

App: Horoufi — com.rezk.horoufi
Developer: Ahmed Rezk
Contact: devahmed1982.ar@gmail.com

2. What we collect: nothing, to us

The app uploads nothing to any server of ours. We operate no server that receives user data. There is no login, no account, no name, birthdate, email or phone number, no photos, audio, contacts or location, and no leaderboard.

Stored locally on the device only: star count, letters completed, day streak and last-played date, and ad-frequency counters. Uninstalling the app erases all of it, as does the “Erase child's progress” button in the Parent screen.

3. What actually leaves the device

a. Advertising (Google AdMob). AdMob receives technical data including an advertising identifier, IP address, device type, OS and ad interactions. Because this app is child-directed, we enforce child settings with no exceptions: non-personalised ads only, no remarketing or behavioural targeting, and a “G” maximum content rating. See Google's privacy policy.

b. Lesson content updates. The app periodically checks GitHub and jsDelivr for a newer lesson file. The request carries no data about the child — it fetches a static public file — but, as with any internet request, those services see the device's IP address.

4. Children's privacy

This app is directed to children and is handled under Google Play's Families policy and children's privacy laws such as COPPA. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — or from anyone, since the app has no accounts. There is no field in which a child can enter any information about themselves, no chat, no sharing, no social links, and no in-app purchases. All ad SDKs used are on Google's Families Self-Certified Ads SDKs list.

5. Parents' rights

Erase progress via Parent screen → Erase child's progress, or uninstall the app. Reset the advertising identifier via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. We cannot delete a specific user's data on request because we never store any.

6. Permissions

One permission only: INTERNET, used for ads and content updates. All learning works fully offline. No camera, microphone, location, contacts or file access.

7. Retention

We retain nothing because we receive nothing. On-device data persists until you erase it. Data collected by Google is subject to Google's own retention periods.

8. Changes

If what is collected changes, we will update this page and the date above, and note any material change in the Google Play release notes.
